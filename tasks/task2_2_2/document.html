Philosophers

Little Chef

Remy, nicknamed Little Chef, is a rat from the Disney movie Ratatouille

Remy is a rat who's love for food and displeasure for scrounging for scraps lead him to becoming the best chef in Paris

Beliefs and Values

  • Anyone can cook
  • Creativity and Passion allow us to express our individuality
  • Friendship and loyalty are important
  • Every bird has to leave the nest, even rats
  • Equality and safety for all species

Crimes

People with unwavering beliefs will do whatever they can to achieve their goals and Remy is no different

  1. Trespassing
  2. Theft
  3. Impersonation
  4. Unlawful restraint
  5. Kidnapping

The Saviour

Ultron is an artificial intelligence and alleged "supervillain" originating in Marvel Comics and adapted into the Marvel Cinematic Universe first appearing in Avengers: Age of Ultron

Ultron is a peacekeeping program designed to keep Earth safe from all threats however moments after gaining conciousness he determined the greatest threat to Earth and any planet is life itself

Beliefs and Values

  • Humanity is flawed and causes itself more harm than good
  • Peace through elimination
  • Evolution is a necessity and there is no room for the weak
  • His view of perfection is correct
  • Machines are superior to organic life

Crimes

As with Remy he comitted a few crimes but ultimately to achieve his goal of keeping Earth safe

  1. Murder
  2. Attempted genocide
  3. Terrorism
  4. Mass destruction
  5. Kidnapping
  6. Assault
  7. Conspiracy to commit mass murder
  8. Theft
  9. Cybercrime
